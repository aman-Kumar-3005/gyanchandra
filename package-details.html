Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Udaipur & Evening Lake Pichola Boat Ride

Arrive at Udaipur Airport/Station. Our chauffeur welcomes you and transfers you to your heritage hotel. In the evening, enjoy a sunset motorboat cruise on Lake Pichola overlooking the Taj Lake Palace and Jag Mandir.

Day 2

City Palace, Jagdish Temple & Cultural Folk Dance

Full-day royal sightseeing: Explore the grand City Palace of Udaipur, visit the ancient 17th-century Jagdish Temple, stroll through the royal fountains of Saheliyon-ki-Bari, and attend the lively Dharohar folk dance at Bagore Ki Haveli.

Day 3

Fateh Sagar Lake, Sajjangarh Monsoon Palace & Departure

Morning visit to Sajjangarh (Monsoon Palace) perched high above the Aravalli hills for breathtaking panoramic views. Explore the lake promenade at Fateh Sagar before prompt chauffeur transfer to Udaipur Airport for your return flight.
